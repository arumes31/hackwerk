# FullCalendar browser assets

The files prefixed `fullcalendar-` in `static/` are the unmodified global browser distribution of FullCalendar Standard 7.0.2 and its Classic theme. They are loaded locally at runtime; the project uses neither Node/npm nor a CDN dependency.

Source URLs:

- `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/fullcalendar@7.0.2/all/global.min.js`
- `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/fullcalendar@7.0.2/themes/classic/global.min.js`
- `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/fullcalendar@7.0.2/skeleton.css`
- `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/fullcalendar@7.0.2/themes/classic/theme.css`
- `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/fullcalendar@7.0.2/themes/classic/palette.css`
